Yamagata City

Yamagata City is known as a central hub for the Shinkansen system leading from Tokyo to the North. Here travelers can experience Ginzan Onsen, an onsen town in the mountains. Yamagata is also known for its surrounding fields and mountines.

Although Yamagata City is a small city, it hosts a bus hub, a university, and the Shinkansen. Here you can travel anywhere with relative ease in the Tohoku region of Japan. All it takes is a 3 hour bus ride that provides free WiFi and luxury travel.
