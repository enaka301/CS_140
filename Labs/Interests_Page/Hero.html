  • Profile

Masamune was a feudal lord in the Edo period of Japan. He is notable for wearing jet black armor and donning a crescent-moon on his helmet. Now his story is portrayed in historical dramas, TV shows, cartoons, and other forms of media.

    Attire

  • Wore a black suit of armor. Similar to Darth Vader. He ended up submitting to Tokugawa.

    • Headquarters

  • Sendai, Miyagi became his base of operations.

    • Popularity

  • Is popular thanks to Japanese media and globally known as the "One-eyed Dragon".
