In conjunction with my profile page, I present to you a new page about the man of the quote, Masamune Date!


Masamune Date was a samurai in old Japan and a daimyo. He was a feudal lord in the Tohoku region. Known for his jet black armor and crescent-moon helmet, he founded Sendai City.

I started my mission in Japan. I remember seeing the towering castles in the countryside and seeing various statues of the warlords who fought in feudal Japan. One samurai that always came about in discussion was Masamune as he was often depicted as a larger than life figure.
