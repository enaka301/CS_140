About Tohoku

  Japan has many different regions. Most frequented by foreigners are the Kansai and Kanto regions. These areas are known for their metropolices, Osaka and Tokyo, entertainment areas, and temples. Many first time Japan tourists put these locations at the top of their Japan trip itineraries.

  Experienced travelers tend to explore outside these areas. Tohoku is the areas north of Tokyo. These are the mountainous, snowy, country side of Japan. It is riddled in deep history, castles, farms, and dialects.
