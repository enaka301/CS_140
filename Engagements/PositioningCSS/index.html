Answers to Questions

What is normal flow in HTML?
It is the natural flow and orientation for a website page.
What does float do? Why do you need to clear the float?
Float allows elements to move to the left or right of a container while wrapping itself in the elements of others. Clear is used to help fix the broken flow of the page caused by float.
What do absolute, fixed, and relative do?
Relative keeps the element in a fixed spot. Fixed keeps the element on the window not the page. Absolute moves according to other elements but stays fixed.
