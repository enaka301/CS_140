Questions and Answers

What are the positives and negatives of hosting your videos on YouTube?
I can choose to have my product being shown without any competitors' ads being shown. I can also host any videos for free. Downside is YouTube will still put links to other competitors.
What is the most useful thing you can do with a transform?
Most useful thing to do with transform is resizing and relocating a figure without losing parts. Also, the video mentioned transform is used for animations.
What is the difference between a transform and a transition?
Transform changes the orientation of a figure or element. Transition changes the css properties of an element over time.
