Answers to Questions

What is the difference between block tags and inline tags?
Block tags are mainly structural and start on a new line. Inline wraps the content on the text and only takes what it needs.
What is the margin, border, and padding?
They are layout layers for a website. Margin is the outer most, then border, followed by padding between elements.
What are units of measurement you can use in CSS?
For most things you can use px.
